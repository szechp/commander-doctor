SWAPS
1. CUT Blasphemous Act -> ADD Chaos Warp | 13-damage symmetric wipe kills your own go-wide goblin board; Chaos Warp answers any permanent at instant speed, filling the missing catch-all slot.
2. CUT Mauhúr, Uruk-hai Captain -> ADD Mayhem Devil | Mauhúr only boosts +1/+1 counters, which the goblin combo barely uses; Devil pings on every sacrifice, creatures and Treasures, turning each outlet into damage.
3. CUT Shadowblood Ridge -> ADD Bloodstained Mire | Filter land produces nothing without other mana; Mire fetches either colour (Blood Crypt included). Land swaps together lift black sources 17→21.
4. CUT March from the Black Gate -> ADD Goblin Recruiter | March only amasses Orc Armies, off the goblin plan; Recruiter stacks Krenko, Kiki-Jiki and other goblins on top for Snoop, Muxus or draws.
5. CUT Chandra's Ignition -> ADD Munitions Expert | Five-mana sorcery that also kills your own goblins; Expert, a two-mana flash Goblin, deals damage equal to your goblin count to a creature or planeswalker.
6. CUT Thrill of Possibility -> ADD Village Rites | Thrill costs a card and two mana; owned Village Rites sacrifices a spare token instead, for one mana, and triggers your death payoffs.
7. CUT First Day of Class -> ADD Deadly Rollick | First Day is a one-turn haste trick (Warchief and Greaves already cover haste); Rollick is your playgroup core staple: instant exile, free with Uglúk out.
8. CUT Faithless Looting -> ADD Rakdos Charm | Looting is card disadvantage with no graveyard plan; Charm adds the deck's only graveyard exile (a coverage gap) plus instant artifact removal.
9. CUT Mordor Muster -> ADD Black Market Connections | Muster is a one-shot Orc Army cantrip; Connections offers a Treasure, card and/or 3/2 changeling Goblin every turn, replacing the cut draw.
10. CUT Mountain -> ADD Sulfurous Springs | Untapped every turn and taps for either colour; black sources are the mana base's weak point (17 unconditional, Phyrexian Arena 54% on curve).
11. CUT Mountain -> ADD Prismatic Vista | Fetch for either basic, adding a black source without losing red; red sources stay at 24 for Kiki-Jiki's and Krenko's pips.
12. CUT Krenko's Command -> ADD Goblin Instigator | Same two bodies for two mana, but owned Instigator is a Goblin card: Matron, Recruiter, Snoop, Lackey, Muxus and Kiki-Jiki can use it.
13. CUT Empty the Warrens -> ADD Goblin-town Flunkies | Four mana for two tokens at typical storm counts; owned Flunkies is a two-mana Goblin card plus a Goblin Army, findable by tutors and Muxus.
14. CUT Furystoke Giant -> ADD Deflecting Swat | Redundant five-mana payoff (Purphoros, Tremors, Kreat, Bombardment cover it); core-staple Swat redirects removal aimed at Krenko or Kiki-Jiki, free with Uglúk.
15. CUT Mountain -> ADD Blazemire Verge | Always untapped: black alone, red once you control a Swamp or Mountain; one more black source for turn-one/two black spells.
ASSUMPTIONS: I took the YAML's bracket 3, threshold 6, goblins theme and gameplan as confirmed, and protected the gameplan's combo cards plus Staff of Domination and Aggravated Assault as if pinned. The Commander Spellbook combo check was skipped because refreshing it writes a cache file. Game Changers stay at 2 of 3. Per playgroup.yaml I used no off-colour fetches and no original duals, and preferred owned spares where they were as good. I checked the new 100 in memory without writing files: the batch is accepted, the coverage gaps are closed, instant answers go 1→4, removal 5→8 and black sources 17→21; ramp is still 9 of 10 and the survival window 8 of 12.
